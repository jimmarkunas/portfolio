## Business context
DIRECTV needed a better way to monetize premium content and sports programming across digital channels.

## The problem
Campaign execution was fragmented across systems and teams, which slowed launches and limited revenue capture.

## What I owned
I led the cross-functional delivery layer across product, business, engineering, and campaign stakeholders.

## What I did
- Unified the delivery motion across business, product, and engineering.
- Reduced launch friction across legacy dependencies and digital channels.
- Created a clearer path from campaign concept to execution.

## Results
- Supported $55M in Q4 upsell revenue.
- Reduced time-to-value by 60 days.
- Enabled coordinated omni-channel execution across STB, web, and mobile.

## Systems / methods
- campaign execution, cross-functional delivery, legacy modernization, roadmap and launch coordination

## What this proves
This case shows enterprise delivery leadership tied directly to commercial impact.
