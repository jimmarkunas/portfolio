## Business context
American Apparel needed digital and store operations to work as one commerce system during a high-pressure period for the business.

## The problem
Regional ecommerce, store inventory, and omni-channel execution were fragmented and commercially limiting.

## What I owned
I helped lead the transformation work across ecommerce, stores, and operational integration layers.

## What I did
- Supported replatforming across regional ecommerce properties.
- Connected stores into a more unified omni-channel model.
- Improved commerce execution tied to inventory and shrink outcomes.

## Results
- Contributed to 43%+ digital revenue growth.
- Supported 14%+ store sales lift.
- Reduced shrink by 65%.

- omni-channel commerce, replatforming, store integration, retail operations
