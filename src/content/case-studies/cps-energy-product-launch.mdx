## Business context
CPS Energy needed a more modern way to manage and communicate streetlight repairs and service status.

## The problem
Repair coordination was slow, customer communication was weak, and operations were burdened by avoidable calls and truck rolls.

## What I owned
I led product and delivery work across stakeholders, service operations, and launch coordination.

## What I did
- Defined and coordinated the launch structure for the new service experience.
- Improved the repair-tracking and service communication workflow.
- Aligned operational execution with the product rollout.

## Results
- Reduced repair-related calls by 73%.
- Improved repair windows from roughly three weeks to one to four days.
- Reduced truck rolls by 43%.

## Systems / methods
- service operations, launch planning, stakeholder coordination, workflow improvement

## What this proves
This case shows product and program leadership in a public-facing operational environment.
