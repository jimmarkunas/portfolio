Why avoid dashboard chart grids?

Your brief explicitly rejects generic SaaS aesthetics. Case studies should lead with outcomes, architecture, timeline logic, and operational proof.

Yes. Skeleton and rhythm stay consistent, while module mix and artifact depth can vary by story and source evidence.
