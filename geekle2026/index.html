Talk your client out of a bad decision with a gamified decision tree

Geekle 2026 presentation by Jim Markunas on using practical workflows to deliver interactive product demos quickly.

Why this talk matters

Use free(ish) Tools to make a gamified, interactive app in under 20 minutes to drive executive decision-making.

Case study anchor

Modere was a technologically complex MLM that wanted to scale from $500m GMV to $1B GMV. The C-Suite was frugal, and the old guard didn't want any technological change at all.

Goal

Drive key technology decisions to get them to $1B + illustrate financial consequences of not changing.

What attendees learn

The talk shows how to use accessible tools, narrative framing, and lightweight code workflows to turn a hard executive conversation into an interactive decision-making experience.

  • It's design-oriented & uses (mostly) free tools
  • It's fun! It turns hard conversations into a game
  • It's provable - Modere scaled to $1b using this system
