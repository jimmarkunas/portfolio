Palette

Primary color, three accents, and contrast-band colors

The system stays light for reading, then uses darker section bands for proof, metrics, and CTA moments. Accent colors are for text highlights, pills, tags, and subtle emphasis—not for painting entire pages.

Overall direction

What the finished site should feel like

Light, calm, structured, and premium—then selectively more dramatic in proof bands and feature sections. Modern without looking trendy. Strong enough for clients. Readable enough for recruiters.

Do

Light-first reading surfaces

Use white and soft-gray surfaces for case studies, resumes, and long-form reading.

Do

Dark proof sections

Use navy/slate bands for metrics, testimonials, awards, and CTA moments.

Avoid

Dark everywhere

Do not turn every page into a moody dashboard. Reading should stay effortless.
