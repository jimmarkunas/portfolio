# Method — From Cult Brand to Repeatable DTC Engine

## Overview
Method had strong retail recognition but no serious direct-to-consumer home. The real strategic question was whether SC Johnson's new stack could scale beyond a single brand.

The project turned a one-brand architecture into a reusable multi-brand engine while still giving Method a flagship consumer experience that felt true to the brand.

## Snapshot
- **Client:** SC Johnson / Method
- **Role:** Sales Engineer & Solutions Architect
- **Timeline:** 2020
- **Services:** Sales Engineering, Solutioning, Architecture
- **Source page:** https://www.notion.so/2d03c5a0592681fc8267ec95e6255307

## Numeric Callouts

- 3 launches (US, CA, UK)
- 20%+ DTC revenue uplift
- 1 unified platform

## The Challenge (Risks / Blockers)
- Method needed a designed, brand-right DTC experience rather than a generic rollout.
- The platform had to work across multiple markets while reusing a common enterprise stack.
- Stakeholders needed proof that the SC Johnson architecture was truly reusable, not just barely working for one launch.

## My Role
I handled sales engineering and solution architecture responsibilities, bridging the business vision, platform design, and delivery reality.

The core value was turning a fragile single-brand pattern into a repeatable multi-brand engine.

## What I Built (Architecture / Integration)
- Reused and adapted the BigCommerce + SAP + Salsify + 3PL stack for Method and Method Men.
- Designed the architecture to support the US, UK, and Canada without fragmenting the platform.
- Kept SCJ, agencies, and vendors aligned around one system rather than spawning new exceptions.

## Delivery Timeline
- Launched Method across three markets.
- Extended the enterprise DTC pattern from Mrs. Meyer’s into a more clearly reusable multi-brand model.
- Balanced brand-led experience quality with platform discipline.

## Outcomes
- Three market launches.
- 20%+ DTC revenue uplift.
- A stronger proof point that SCJ could run one shared DTC platform across brands.

## Proof
- Companion case to Mrs. Meyer’s showing architecture reuse
- Strong evidence for multi-brand DTC and enterprise solutioning
