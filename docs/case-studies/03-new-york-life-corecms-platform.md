# New York Life — COREcms Multi-Tenant Platform

## Overview
New York Life needed a regulated-enterprise version of a site builder: something that let thousands of agents launch and manage sites quickly without losing brand, legal, or compliance control.

The product became more than a one-off client build. It became the basis of a reusable multi-tenant platform that Fusion92 could take to other enterprise clients.

## Numeric Callouts

- 2 weeks → 3 days review cycle
- 6 months → 3 weeks time-to-launch for compliant sites
- 12,000+ agent sites

## The Challenge (Risks / Blockers)
- The platform had to support scale across 12,000+ agents while preserving central governance.
- Content, design, analytics, and approval workflows all needed to behave consistently in a regulated environment.
- The experience had to be usable by non-technical teams, not just specialists.
- The product also needed to support AI-driven and analytics-enabled workflows without creating compliance exposure.

## My Role
I owned the product layer that turned the work from a custom client solution into a repeatable platform.

That included shaping the site-builder experience, the multi-tenant model, the content and approval workflows, and the roadmap logic behind the product.

## What I Built (Architecture / Integration)
- Defined a multi-tenant WYSIWYG site-builder model with centralized governance.
- Built in approval workflows, reusable content, and role-based control so agents could move faster without breaking compliance.
- Positioned the product as an enterprise-grade platform rather than a one-off site factory.
- Integrated AI-driven UI/UX capabilities where they improved usability and workflow efficiency.

## Delivery Timeline
- Shipped the product across New York Life's agent ecosystem.
- Reduced legal/compliance review cycle time and cut launch timelines dramatically.
- Created a stronger product story and platform foundation for Fusion92's broader market use.

## Outcomes
- Review cycles dropped from two weeks to three days.
- Time to launch compliant sites dropped from six months to three weeks.
- The solution scaled to 12,000+ agent sites.
- Fusion92 gained a reusable enterprise platform rather than a single-client deliverable.

## Proof

- Measurable reduction in review and launch time
