# LEGO — Global Shop Transformation via BCG

## Overview
LEGO was deep into a broad digital and enterprise transformation, but the online shop still lagged the strength of the underlying global platform.

The goal was to turn that abstract transformation into a flagship commerce experience that felt worthy of the brand while still fitting the realities of a complex SAP-centered enterprise.

## Snapshot
- **Client:** Boston Consulting Group / LEGO

- **Timeline:** June 2015 – June 2016
- **Services:** Product Management, Programme Management, Architecture

## Numeric Callouts

- 3x revenue uplift
- $50M+ cost savings
- 33% operating-margin improvement

## The Challenge (Risks / Blockers)
- The back-end platform was strong, but the front-end experience felt cluttered and dated.
- Navigation, cart, and checkout had grown organically and varied across regions.
- Mobile and tablet experiences lagged even as more families shopped across devices.
- The new design had to respect enterprise platform constraints while still feeling unmistakably LEGO.

## My Role
I owned architecture and PRDs for the most complex user flows, including navigation, cart, checkout, and promotions.

That meant translating enterprise platform realities into simple, consistent experiences that design, engineering, and business could all support.

## What I Built (Architecture / Integration)
- Re-architected the flagship shop experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Simplified and rationalized complex browsing and checkout patterns.
- Anchored the front-end redesign in the realities of the global enterprise platform instead of designing in a vacuum.

## Delivery Timeline
- Produced detailed product and architecture work for the most complex flows.
- Aligned business, design, and engineering around a more coherent commerce model.
- Helped make LEGO.com feel like a flagship rather than an afterthought.
