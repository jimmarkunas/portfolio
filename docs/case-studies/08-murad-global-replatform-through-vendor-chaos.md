# Murad — Shipping a Global Re-Platform Through Vendor Chaos

## Overview
Murad's re-platform was not a clean migration. It was an understaffed global DTC program with a brittle Oracle backbone, subscriptions in scope, and multiple agency failures already in flight.

The real job was to force clarity into a politically bruised program, document how Oracle, BigCommerce, payments, and subscriptions should actually work, and still get the launch over the line.

## Numeric Callouts

- 6 markets launched
- 0 Magento dev team required for the new operating model
- 3 custom BigCommerce integrations

## The Challenge (Risks / Blockers)
- Murad had already burned through one agency and was in the process of firing a second.
- The launch date did not move, even as vendors rotated and documentation stayed weak.
- Subscriptions were non-negotiable, but key platform integrations were either incomplete or missing.
- A late-discovered payments settlement gap threatened finance, reconciliation, subscriptions, and the overall launch.

## My Role
I served as the person holding the delivery, architecture, and integration story together.

That meant translating a vague 'make BigCommerce work with Oracle and subscriptions' mandate into a documented, repeatable model the business could actually operate.

## What I Built (Architecture / Integration)
- Defined the target integration model across BigCommerce, Oracle EBS, subscriptions, and core communications tooling.
- Reduced the dependency on a large Magento-specific team by moving toward a leaner, self-service operating model.
- Created a reusable delivery pattern that could support future markets instead of a one-off launch.
- De-risked the program by turning tribal knowledge and agency assumptions into explicit flows and responsibilities.

## Delivery Timeline
- Re-platformed Murad across the US, UK, AU, DE, MX, and MY.
- Aligned multiple agencies, internal teams, and vendor assumptions under one delivery path.
- Closed critical integration and payments gaps late in the program to preserve launch viability.
- Shipped a subscriptions-capable stack tied into Oracle as financial source of truth.

## Outcomes
- Launched in six markets.
- Removed dependence on a full Magento operations model for day-to-day change.
- Delivered a documented pattern the business could reuse instead of repeating the same chaos market by market.
