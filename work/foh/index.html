Problem Statement

A Dead Brand Needed a Live Campaign Machine

Frederick's went bankrupt and shuttered its stores. ABG bought the brand as an online revival play, not a nostalgia project. The relaunch had to be fast, marketing-led, and lean - sustainable without a bloated enterprise stack. The challenge was building a DTC flagship that could handle celebrity-driven traffic spikes, constant campaign launches, and a full rebrand, while Shopify Plus was still maturing as an enterprise platform and needed creative workarounds behind the scenes.

Megan Fox wasn't just a face on a campaign. She was a co-owner and the primary commercial catalyst - the reason the relaunch had cultural relevance and press traction. That kind of attention is an asset until your stack folds under it. The commerce infrastructure had to survive the traffic and keep running between campaigns, not just survive launch day.

Shopify Plus was the right call on cost and launch speed. But the clean sales story didn't always match the implementation reality at the time. The platform needed someone who understood where the gaps were and how to work around them without over-engineering the solution. The job was equal parts architecture judgment and practical problem-solving - knowing what to build, what to defer, and what to skip entirely.
