Context & Stakes

Mrs. Meyer's was a cult-favorite brand with zero direct channel. SC Johnson depended entirely on Target, Walmart, and other retailers for both sales and customer insight. There was no owned relationship with end customers, no way to test bundles or subscriptions, and no first-party data to inform marketing or product decisions. Operationally, everything was tuned for pallets, not parcels: SAP and the LiveArea/Merkle WMS were built to ship truckloads to retailers, not thousands of individual consumer orders.

Technical Blockers

  • No DTC storefront or consumer checkout
  • SAP not configured for parcel-level orders
  • WMS built for retail pallet fulfillment only
  • No tax, fraud, or consumer payment layer

Business Blockers

  • Had to prove DTC wouldn't cannibalize retail
  • Multiple agencies + SCJ needed alignment
  • Architecture had to be reusable for next brands
  • No existing first-party data or CRM foundation

Success Criteria: Launch US & Canada DTC. Deliver 15%+ incremental revenue lift. Prove the stack is reusable for Caldrea and Method without rebuilding from scratch.

What I Owned

Architecture & Integration

  • Designed full integration model: BC, SAP, Salsify, WMS
  • Chose and configured Avalara, Braintree/Kount, Klaviyo
  • Negotiated API terms with Google & Braintree directly
  • Defined data ownership and sync flows

Program & Relationships

  • Managed SCJ, agencies & BigCommerce alignment
  • Flew to Chicago to resolve SCJ stakeholder friction
  • Pushed for Rewind backups — saved launch after data wipe
  • Acted as "adult in the room" across all vendors

Key Decision: Treat this as a reusable template from day one — not a one-off launch. Every architectural call was filtered through "will this still make sense when we plug in Caldrea and Method?"

The Solution

Commerce & Catalog

  • BigCommerce storefront for US & Canada
  • Salsify → BC for product content & imagery
  • SAP → BC for core product & pricing data
  • Tri-directional sync: Salsify, SAP, BC aligned

Checkout, Tax & Payments

  • Avalara for real-time tax calculation
  • PayPal/Braintree for payments
  • Kount fraud tools built into payment flow
  • Simple checkout without separate fraud stack

Fulfillment & Logistics

  • Orders: BC → SAP → LiveArea/Merkle WMS
  • Same warehouse backbone as retail, no rebuild
  • Parcel-level pick, pack & ship w/o new playbook
  • Rewind backups saved catalog before launch

Customer Data & CRM

  • Klaviyo for transactional & lifecycle email
  • Google Analytics for site & funnel performance
  • First-party data from day one
  • Subscription-ready architecture for future phases

January 2019: Project Kickoff

Mapped SCJ's SAP and WMS landscape and defined the integration architecture across BigCommerce, SAP, Salsify, and LiveArea/Merkle WMS.

March 2019: Method Starts

Method kicked off as a parallel workstream while Mrs. Meyer's US build and integrations continued.

July 2019: Rewind Save + Mrs. Meyer's US Launch

The catalog was accidentally wiped pre-launch; Rewind backups restored everything, and Mrs. Meyer's US launched in the same month.

September 2019: Method Complete

Method's 6-month parallel track completed, validating the shared architecture under overlapping delivery timelines.
