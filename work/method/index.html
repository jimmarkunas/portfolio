Context & Stakes

Method had a strong retail presence and packaging everyone recognized, but no real direct-to-consumer home. SC Johnson had just proven a new BigCommerce + SAP stack with Mrs. Meyer's. The challenge: make Method the second brand on that platform without breaking what was already working, and do it in a way that made the third and fourth brand faster, not harder.

Platform-Side Blockers

  • Stack was new and still being validated

  • Localized pricing, tax, and shipping per region
  • Risk of breaking Mrs. Meyer's if done wrong

The Brief: Make Method feel like its own world, but do it on the same shared back-end so SCJ could keep adding brands without rebuilding from scratch.

The Solution

Implementation & Integration
