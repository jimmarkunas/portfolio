The Problem I Solved

Modere's revenue ambitions had outgrown its technology. The company was running a billion-dollar direct sales operation on a legacy, home-grown platform that was expensive to maintain, hard to change, and brittle under scale. Leadership was clear: they did not believe they could get past the $500M plateau without a complete rethink of their digital stack.

Affiliate workflows were equally painful. There was no modern mobile app, no reliable shared-cart motion across channels, and launching new markets or promotions behaved like custom projects. Central teams were trapped in ticket queues for products, pricing, promos, and content changes across systems that did not agree on core truths like inventory, eligibility, and pricing.

Key Decision: I kept BigCommerce intentionally narrow as a headless transaction layer, with Pimcore as product brain and Contentstack as content layer, so Modere could scale and swap services without re-platforming again.

The Solution

We built a MACH-style, API-first stack with BigCommerce as the transaction layer, Contentstack for content, Pimcore as the product brain, a custom promotion engine for MLM-specific commission/eligibility rules, and payment/payout integrations across Braintree, Klarna, and PayQuicker on Azure + Kubernetes.

Business Capability Delivered

  • Single product/promo core serving 10+ markets and 7 languages
  • Business-managed content and catalog updates without waiting on engineering
  • Composable integrations that allow service-level replacement without re-platforming

Implementation Highlights

  • Product data, bundles, and regional variations mastered in Pimcore
  • Content and campaign publishing managed in Contentstack
  • Promotion engine applied eligibility + payout rules outside core checkout
  • MobiLoud wrapped web experience into one localized iOS/Android app footprint

Why it worked: SaaS handled commodity functions while custom services handled Modere's unique MLM complexity, creating a stack that was both modern and operationally practical.
