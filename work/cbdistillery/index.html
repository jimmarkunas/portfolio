Context & Stakes

CBDistillery wasn't coming to BigCommerce for a nicer theme. They were coming because their payments life had blown up. On WooCommerce, they were already straining the limits of a non-enterprise stack. Then their payment provider dropped them. Overnight, a $15M brand was at risk of not being able to process cards at all—and without solving payments first, nothing else mattered.

The Challenge

Payments Blockers

  • Existing processor dropped them — no warning
  • 10+ banks and gateways refused their volume
  • No vaulting = no subscriptions = no recurring revenue
  • Predatory reserves and arbitrary volume caps from the few willing to talk

Platform Blockers

  • WooCommerce couldn't scale to enterprise traffic and volume
  • No native Square + BigCommerce tokenized integration existed
  • OrderGroove subscriptions depended on reliable card vaulting
  • Finance needed clean settlement reporting without manual gymnastics

The Core Problem: Without a processor willing to underwrite CBDistillery for the long term, there was no point discussing site design, UX, or campaigns. Payments were the existential problem — and that's where I started.

What I Owned

Fintech Negotiation & Deal Architecture

  • Personally worked through 10+ banks, gateways, and processors
  • Negotiated Square deal: no predatory reserves, no arbitrary volume caps
  • Secured a 3-year commitment so campaigns could be planned without fear of being dropped
  • Contract terms and risk rules negotiated upfront with acquiring banks

Integration Lead: BC + Square + OrderGroove

  • Led technical design for all three systems: flows, data mappings, edge cases
  • Pushed BC engineering to prioritize the tokenized Square integration
  • Designed unified token vault across one-time, subscription, and repeat orders
  • Built retry logic and dunning rules into the same payment rails

Risk & Compliance Architecture

  • Enforced product-level restrictions within the payment flow
  • Gave acquiring banks the KYC and reporting they needed to stay comfortable
  • Designed controls so CBDistillery could grow without triggering review
  • Zero predatory terms — non-negotiable from day one

Reusable Blueprint Thinking

  • Treated this as a reference pattern, not a one-off rescue
  • Documented flows BC and OrderGroove could reuse for other high-risk merchants
  • Integration architecture designed so future complex merchants could plug in
  • BC now points to CBDistillery as the CBD reference pattern
