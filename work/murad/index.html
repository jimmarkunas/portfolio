Context & Stakes

When I stepped in, Murad's DTC business ran on a heavy Magento + Oracle stack that only moved when a small army of developers pushed it. Leadership wanted BigCommerce—lower cost, self-sufficient marketing—without disturbing Oracle as the financial source of truth. But the project was already bruised: one agency burned, a second being fired, a third just onboarding, and the launch date hadn't moved.

Technical Blockers

  • BC–OrderGroove subscription integration unbuilt
  • SendGrid native BC integration missing
  • BC couldn't settle payments over API (found 3 weeks out)
  • Oracle flows undocumented, partly tribal knowledge

Organizational Blockers

  • Third agency just onboarded mid-project
  • No single owner of integration contracts
  • Demanding stakeholder group, cultural complexity
  • Subscriptions were non-negotiable at launch

The Real Problem: It wasn't just re-platforming a cosmetics brand. It was taking a politically tense, under-specified program with rotating vendors and forcing it over the line in a way Murad could reuse for future markets.
