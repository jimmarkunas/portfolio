Mandate

Build a governed multi-tenant platform that let New York Life launch and manage 12,000+ agent sites at speed without creating legal exposure, brand chaos, or agent retention risk.

What I Changed

Defined the multi-tenant product model and permission architecture that gave agents speed without losing central control. Built a proprietary AI compliance automation tool that cut review time 40%. Structured the platform so it became a reusable product. Connected Azure IDP/SSO, MuleSoft, AEM, Salesforce, and Google My Business into one governed content and lead generation operation.

Outcome

Site launches cut from 6 months to 2 weeks. Compliance review time cut 40% via AI automation. 12,000+ sites governed centrally. 200% lead uplift across the agent network. The product model became the foundation of Fusion92's CORECMS.

Problem Statement

Too Much Legal Risk

Before the CORECMS platform, each agent website behaved like its own product. Local vendors, inconsistent branding, manual compliance checks, and no single source of truth made every update slow, expensive, and risky. New York Life needed a way to support 12,000+ agent sites without letting speed create legal exposure or brand chaos.

New York Life was also fighting an agent retention problem. Without a compliant, high-performing digital presence, agents had every incentive to go independent, taking their books of business with them. A platform that was slow to launch, hard to update, and legally exposed didn't just create operational risk. It made the company easier to leave.

What I Owned

Product, Compliance, and Platform

I owned the product logic, compliance architecture, stakeholder alignment, and delivery accountability for a platform that had to work for 12,000+ sites across a litigation-sensitive regulated industry. The technical work mattered. The governance model mattered more.

  1. 1

    Multi-tenant product model

    Defined the multi-tenant architecture that gave every agent a governed, brand-compliant site without treating each one as a separate project.

  2. 2

    AI compliance automation

    Built a proprietary AI-assisted compliance review tool that automated previously manual content checks. Review cycles dropped 40%.

  3. 3

    Permissions and governance architecture

    Designed the role-based permission model that matched New York Life's actual operating structure: what agents could publish, what required home office review, and what was locked at the brand level.

  4. 4

    Stakeholder alignment across a bureaucratic org

    Led backlog grooming, workshops, demos, and steering conversations across home office, digital, compliance, and legal stakeholders. In a company of New York Life's size and regulatory sensitivity, keeping everyone aligned was crucial.

  5. 5

    Platform delivery and lead gen performance

    Connected Azure IDP/SSO, MuleSoft, AEM, Salesforce, and Google My Business into one governed content and lead generation system. Site launch time dropped from 6 months to 2 weeks. Lead and appointment activity across the agent network improved 200%.

Solution

One Platform, Tight Guardrails, AI-Assisted

A governed multi-tenant CMS that gave agents the speed they needed without removing the compliance controls New York Life required. AI-assisted review automation sat inside the workflow so compliance checks happened faster without more headcount.
