Problem

The prior PM had burned the timeline, fractured stakeholder trust, and left the scope uncontrolled. Mandate

Replace the prior PM, stabilize the engagement, define a global reference storefront across 5 countries, and manage the Oracle-to-SAP migration in parallel without letting it stall or break the commerce program.

Rebuilt delivery structure and stakeholder trust within 21 days. Defined the global reference storefront that gave all 5 countries one model to follow. Problem Statement

Distressed Program + Live ERP Migration

The program was on fire when I arrived; the prior PM burned the timeline, broke stakeholder trust on both sides of the Atlantic, and left scope uncontrolled across 5 markets. Multi-million-dollar accounts were still ordering by phone, and the client had lost confidence in the delivery team.

I stabilized everything in 21 days: rebuilt the delivery structure, restored trust with US and EU stakeholders, and got the program back on a trackable roadmap. Then, the Oracle-to-SAP migration landed mid-build, on top of a live commerce program across five countries with active cutover sequencing already in motion.

The scale and regulatory complexity made it worse. Five markets, multiple animal species, different regulations for pets and livestock in every country. Every market needed its own catalog, pricing, and ordering flow. No country was allowed to break the program.

Solution

One Global Model, Controlled Local Variation, Parallel ERP

The solution required three things to work simultaneously: a global commerce model that 5 countries could follow, a local variation framework that gave markets flexibility, and an Oracle/SAP ERP coexistence strategy.

Why the architecture worked

Commerce, product data, content, and market logic each had a clear home. That separation reduced custom work, protected the platform from vendor drift, and made expansion repeatable instead of rebuilding market by market.

What the business gained

Markets could manage the right catalog, pricing, and ordering logic without turning routine commercial work into another country-specific build. The global model stayed focused on commerce while local variation remained controlled.
