Context & Stakes

K2 Sports ran 9 global brand sites on Demandware; powerful but fragile, and heading for end-of-life. Avante handled ERP, Quivers handled omni-channel inventory and retailer fulfillment. Marketing loved the brand experiences. IT knew the party was over. The stakes were real: winter sports brands don't get do-overs on seasonal traffic, and any wrong move on domains or redirects would torch years of SEO equity.

What Was Breaking

  • Demandware sunsetting (non-negotiable deadline)
  • Every integration maintained brand by brand
  • Manual reconciliation between storefronts and ERP

The Stakes

  • Winter sports = hard seasonal revenue windows
  • Domain changes = real SEO risk at peak traffic
  • 9 brands had to migrate without a single bad launch

SEO & DNS Routing Strategy

Multi-Brand Template Thinking

  • Integration contracts, DNS pattern, and BC4WordPress all designed for reuse

My Edge: I owned the blueprint that made K2's multi-brand, multi-store setup run as a single engine: a pattern BigCommerce has since reused with other multi-brand clients.

The Solution

Commerce & Content Stack

  • BigCommerce (multi-store) for cart, checkout, and APIs
  • WordPress + Contentstack for front-end experience layer
  • BC4WordPress for headless rendering under clean URLs
  • DNS proxy routing traffic to right regional store per brand

ERP & Fulfillment

  • Avante as master for inventory, orders, and financials
  • Custom BC/Quivers integration for omnichannel fulfillment
  • Orders, returns, and allocations move predictably between BC, Quivers, and Avante
  • No overselling across DTC and retailer channels during peak season
