Chicks With Guns: Building a Digital Music Magazine

In 2009, I founded Chicks With Guns as an online-first music publication built for web, mobile, and video. We grew to 250k+ monthly uniques, launched a native iOS app, built a global staff, and were recognized by Yahoo as a top online music publication.

Problem Statement

Building Credibility at Scale Without Capital

The challenge was proving a digital-native publication could compete with legacy music outlets on quality and access while operating with no budget. We needed high editorial standards, dependable artist relationships, and repeatable distribution across multiple channels without paid acquisition.

I Ran CWG as a Product + Publication

CWG succeeded because we treated content operations like product operations. Coverage planning, production workflow, quality control, and distribution were managed as one system across web, mobile, and video.

I recruited and led a volunteer team through mission, standards, and opportunity. We built trust with labels & artists through reliability & execution, which gave us access that money couldn't buy.

Solution

A Multi-Surface Editorial Engine

We designed CWG as one coordinated media system. Editorial, photography, and video were planned once and distributed across every surface with consistent quality and velocity.

How We Operated With No Budget

CWG ran on a distributed contributor model with clear assignment ownership and production standards. The operating principle was simple: quality first, consistency always.

That discipline helped us build repeatable audience growth and maintain artist/label trust despite having none of the budget advantages legacy publications had.

1
Coverage calendars and assignment discipline kept output consistent.
2
Cross-surface asset reuse increased leverage per story.
3
Execution quality protected brand credibility and access.

The hard lesson: attention alone isn't a business model. Sustainable monetization has to be designed intentionally, not bolted on later.

Before & After

CWG transformed from an unfunded concept into a recognized digital publication, and gave me a durable framework for scaling quality under constraints.

Before

Unproven Concept

  • No capital, no legacy brand advantage, and no paid team.
  • Skepticism that online-only outlets could match print credibility.
  • Unclear path to consistent quality across multiple channels.
After

Recognized Platform

  • 250k+ monthly uniques achieved through organic growth.
  • Yahoo recognition validated market credibility.
  • Repeatable operating model for distributed editorial execution.
